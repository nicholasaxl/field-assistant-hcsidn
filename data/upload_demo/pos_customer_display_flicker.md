# HX-POS-200 Customer Display Flicker

Synthetic demo document for testing the upload feature. It is not indexed until you upload it.

## Symptom
The customer-facing display flickers, shows horizontal lines, or turns off for a few seconds during a sale.

## Fix
1. Reseat the display cable at both the terminal and the display head. A half-seated cable is the most common cause.
2. Set the display refresh rate to 60 Hz under Service Menu > Display > Refresh rate.
3. Replace the display cable with a known good one if the flicker continues.
4. Update the display firmware from Service Menu > Firmware > Customer display.
5. Replace the display head and log its serial number if all of the above fail.

## Escalation
Escalate to level 2 if the flicker appears on more than one terminal at the same store, because that points to a power supply problem rather than the display.
