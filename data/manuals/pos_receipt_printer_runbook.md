# HX-POS-200 Receipt Printer Troubleshooting

Synthetic sample runbook for demonstration purposes.

## Paper out or paper jam (P-1001, P-1002)
1. Press the cover release and open the printer cover.
2. Remove any jammed or crumpled paper. Do not pull against the print head.
3. Wipe the platen roller with a lint-free cloth and isopropyl alcohol.
4. Load an 80mm thermal roll so the paper feeds from the bottom of the roll, with the thermal side facing up.
5. Close the cover until it clicks, then run a test print from Service Menu > Printer > Test print.

## Faint or blank receipts
Blank receipts usually mean the paper was loaded upside down, because only one side is thermally coated. Flip the roll and retry. If faint printing continues, clean the print head with an alcohol swab and check the print density setting.

## Print head overheated (P-1003)
Power off the printer for 10 minutes and make sure the vents are not blocked. If the error repeats after cooling, replace the print head. Record the head serial number on the service ticket.

## Cash drawer does not open (P-3001)
The drawer is triggered through the printer. Check that the RJ12 drawer cable is seated at the printer, confirm the drawer-kick setting is enabled under Service Menu > Peripherals, and use the manual key release so the customer is not delayed.
